Manuscript Formatter

Give it a book that already looks the way you want, add your manuscript, and get your words back in that book’s design — ready to upload.

1 Pick your two Word files

The design you want to copy

A Word file that is already laid out the way you want — a KDP interior template, or a book you have formatted before. Only its design is used, never its words.

Drag a Word file here, or click to browse

Your manuscript

The Word file with your actual book in it. Every word is copied across exactly as you wrote it — nothing is reworded, reordered or cut.

Drag a Word file here, or click to browse

Both files need to be Word .docx files. If yours is an older .doc, or a Pages or Google Docs file, open it and choose Save as → Word document first.
