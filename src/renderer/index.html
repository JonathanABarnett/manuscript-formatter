Manuscript Formatter

Start with a KDP template or a book whose layout you like, add your manuscript, and get a new Word file that is most of the way formatted. Give it one final check before uploading to KDP.

  1. Choose a design

    Pick a size and a look, or use a template you already have.

  2. Check the pages

    See your own words laid out at the real size of the book.

  3. Make the file

    A brand-new Word document. Your originals are untouched.

  4. Check, then upload

    Read it in Word, then let KDP’s previewer have the last word.

How your book will look

These samples use your words, page size, margins, fonts, and spacing. They are a guide, not an exact Word preview: page breaks, line wrapping, pictures, headers, footers, and page numbers can look different in the finished file. Always review it in Word.
